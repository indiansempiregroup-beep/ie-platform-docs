# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus on the work, not the individual.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or disruptive behavior is not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from the project community.

## Reporting

Please report concerns to the maintainers through the repository issue tracker or a private channel designated by the team.
